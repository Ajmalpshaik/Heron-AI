# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This project exists for BIM professionals, many of whom are not developers and
should never be made to feel stupid for that.

## Our standards

**Expected:**

- Assume good faith. Most confusion is a documentation problem, not a person problem.
- Explain in the terms the other person works in. A BIM modeller asking about ducts should not be
  answered with a lecture about assembly loading.
- Disagree about ideas, and say why. Technical disagreement is how the architecture gets better.
- Accept that someone else's constraints may be real even when they are not yours — different Revit
  versions, different countries, different client rules, different levels of experience.

**Not acceptable:**

- Personal attacks, insults, or dismissiveness about someone's technical level
- Harassment of any kind, public or private
- Publishing someone else's private information
- Sharing anyone's client project data, in issues, pull requests or discussions
- Sustained disruption of discussions

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns privately to the maintainer. Reports are handled confidentially.

Responses range from a private word, to editing or removing content, to a temporary or permanent ban —
proportionate to what happened.
