### What being DRAFT costs these reads - measured 2026-11-11, not estimated

*Every figure in this section was measured with fourteen cards DRAFT, `report-family-forms` among them. It is
PROVEN again since 2026-11-12 and none of the figures was taken again; none of the sentences quoted is that
