### Context

D-163 docked Heron's recent changes in Revit, each row with Select, Zoom and Undo, and item 5 sent every
button through the one dispatcher, looking at the model hold. A chat holds a model for
`bridge.modelHoldMinutes` (3 by default) after its last request there ([D-141](../decisions/D-141.md)), and the
pane's requests carry no conversation, so Select and Zoom pressed on a change a chat had just kept were
refused *"in use by another chat"* for those three minutes - exactly when a modeller looks at it
([row 5b-1825](../fragment-issues/row-5b-1825.md)).

### Decision

1. **Select and Zoom from the pane are not refused by a chat's model hold.** They change the selection and
   the view, never the model, and they are the modeller's own clicks in Revit.
2. **They still go through the one dispatcher** (D-163 item 5) and still never take the model from a chat:
   the hold stays the chat's, and its other requests keep it.
3. **Nothing else looks past a hold.** Every request that changes the model, from a chat or the pane, meets
   the hold as before; the pane's Undo follows [D-175](../decisions/D-175.md).

### Alternatives considered

- **Leave them waiting out the hold.** Declined: the buttons would be dead in the three minutes after every
  change, when they are wanted.

### Consequences

- Row 5b-1825 is built to point 1; NW13 can run as written once it is.
