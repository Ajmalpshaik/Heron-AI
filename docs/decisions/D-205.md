### Context

Asked to make a question's near-tie go to the read, the session measured first
([`brain/retrieval-history.md`](../../brain/retrieval-history.md), 2026-11-26). With the host's `question` flag,
D-128 already leaves every write out, so his 79 and the 124 plain questions reach no write at all. Without the
flag - the lookup a host gets when it says nothing - 9 of his and 9 of the plain ones reach a write, and most
are not near-ties: *"does this duct have lining"* has its nearest read 13 ranks behind. The one lever left
that reads no words is risk on a near-tie, which every retrieval change before it had left to the owner.

Measured as a scratch patch on `lexical`, 754 fragments, flag off:

| | main | margin 0.61 rank | margin 1 rank |
|---|---|---|---|
| his 79: first / top three | 23 / 33 | 23 / 33 | 22 / 33 |
| his 79: asked for no change, handed one | 9 | 8 | 8 |
| 124 plain questions reaching a write | 9 | 8 | 7 |

At one rank his change request *"Place a door in every room, like the one I placed."* lost its first place to
a read. That is why the owner chose the smaller margin.

### Decision

1. **When the ranked winner is above the question ceiling and a card at or below it scores within the status
   nudge's span of the winner, that card goes first**, and the rest keep their order. The margin is
   `QUALITY_SPAN`, derived from the status table: a gap the status nudge alone could have made, so the two
   routes did not prefer the write. About 0.61 of one fusion rank today.
2. **The line is `question_ceiling()`**, read from the tool registry: the level just below the change door's
   own. A card whose risk cannot be read is never put first as safe.
3. **It reads the declared risks and the scores, never the request's words.** It applies to every ranked
   lookup through `heron_brain.lookup`, question or not. An exact declared phrase (`identity`) and the cache
   are never moved.
4. **The answer says so.** A card moved names the write it was put ahead of, in the lookup's note.

### Alternatives considered

- **One whole rank.** Two plain questions off a write instead of one, and his door request lost first place.
- **Nothing**, leaving the flag-off path as it was. The flag is the full fix where a host sets it; this is the
  floor where one does not.
- **Reading the words** (a leading *what*, a question mark). Against D-01, and D-128 measured it worse.

### Consequences

- **Easy:** a coin toss between a write and a read no longer hands the write first.
- **Hard:** a change request near-tied with a read gets the read first. That costs no first place on his 79 at
  this margin. The write is still in the shortlist, and a ranked guess is handed back to the host either way.
- **Not covered:** callers of `heron_retrieve.find` that pass no `safe`. These are `tools/check-routing.py`,
  `tools/measure-routes.py`, `tools/measure-brain.py` and `heron_context`, and they rank as before.
- **Not tested in Revit.** This is a ranking change; nothing here reaches the add-in.
