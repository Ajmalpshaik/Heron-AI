### Context

D-70 decided Heron keeps a counter of which standards clauses helped, on the PC, as numbers. D-191 set its four
open parts and it was built: each clause shown to the chat was counted as *offered*; the modeller's word, through
the `heron_helped` tool, counted it as *helped*; and helped over offered moved that clause up by at most half of one
fusion rank next time - enough to settle a tie and never to beat a better match.

Asked whether to keep it, the owner said remove it. His reason, in his words: *"if this is not healp full if we keep
no need ... you can remebr [the procedure] but no all the items becose bim work ... daly difrent difrent job ... it
will take tame also ... overlap or mistake"*. A modeller's jobs differ day to day, so a count of which clause helped
last time says little about the next question; asking for and recording it costs time; and a count carried from one
job to another can push the wrong clause up.

### Decision

1. **The usage counter is removed** - its module, its test, the `heron_helped` tool, the count of clauses offered
   that the standards and context answers kept, and the nudge it added in retrieval. Every other served tool keeps
   its name, its arguments and its place in the list.
2. **A clause's place in an answer is fusion's alone** - word match and meaning search, as for a clause that was
   never counted. Documents get no nudge of any kind; fragments keep the status nudge, which this does not touch.
3. **The file the counter wrote is left where it is, unused.** It was `<scope>-usage.json` beside each knowledge
   store, in Heron's knowledge folder (`%USERPROFILE%\.heron\knowledge` on Windows, D-135, and its `projects`
   folder): only clause ids and counts, never a question. Nothing reads it now, and no code was written to delete it:
   it stays on the modeller's PC until he deletes it, and deleting it by hand loses nothing.

### Alternatives considered

- **Keep it.** Not chosen by the owner, for the reasons above.
- **Keep the counting and drop only the nudge, or the other way round.** Either keeps half of the mechanism with
  none of the use he asked for.
- **Delete the counter files on the modeller's PC.** Not done: a file of a modeller's is never deleted by a change
  he did not ask for in those words, and these hold nothing that does harm by staying.

### Consequences

- **On a PC where nobody ever said a clause helped, every standards answer is the one it was.** The nudge was zero
  for a clause with no *helped* count, so only a clause somebody marked can move, and it moves by less than one rank.
- **Served tools are one fewer.** Count them with `python mcp/server/heron_tools.py` rather than reading a figure here.
- **The release builder still refuses a `-usage.json` file**, so a leftover counter never rides in a download.
- **Audit records already written as `knowledge.helped` stay in the audit trail**, as history. None is written now.
- **R-16 and R-25 of [the RAG requirement note](../work-notes/plans/rag/01-requirements.md)** lose the success rate
  D-191 built: for clauses as for fragments, success rate and recency are now signals nothing records.
- **[Q-94](../open-questions/tier-3.md) stays answered** - it was about what a counter may keep, and it is kept as history.
- **Register rows, handover notes and decisions that name the counter are history and keep their words.**
- Nothing here touches Revit. Not run in Revit.
