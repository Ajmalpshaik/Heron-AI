### Context

Claude Code gives a chat only the first 4,096 characters of a server's instructions, and Heron's had grown past it
([row 5b-2080](../fragment-issues/row-5b-2080.md)); pull request #1500 brought them to 3,785, word for word, with no
room left under the budget `tests/test_tool_sizes.py` keeps (the cap less 300 for the off-Windows line). The owner
then wrote rules every modeller should have that Heron's sheet did not carry: work on the selection or the active
view, change only what was asked, keep the model whole, check after a change, short replies.

Article 9 took 1,005 of the 3,785 characters, mostly as developer words - `TransactionGroup`, "superseding D-99's
exception", what the dialog selects and isolates - which say how `revit_change` meets the Article rather than what
the Article requires. Rewording the sheet instead would have put a second copy of every Article in it, which the
Constitution and the registry's copy rule both refuse.

### Decision

1. **Article 9 is reworded in the Constitution itself, in plain words, with every part of its meaning kept:** the
   preview a `MODIFY` operation needs (what will change, how many elements, what will be skipped); that a change from
   a chat is shown in Revit, which asks Keep or Discard, and nothing is kept until Keep, Discard, Esc or closing it
   rolling it back; the one recorded exception, the *Ask to Keep* switch in Heron's Settings, off by default, under which a
   change is kept at once as one named undo entry **and its answer says what it did** - the compensating control the
   exception has always had; and the third link's ten quiet minutes, with a publish and a DRAFT's one run still asked.
   What left the Article is how the add-in does it - one named `TransactionGroup` (Article 8 says that), what Revit
   selects and isolates (D-126 says that), the pinned model (Article 12b says that) - and the history of which decision
   superseded which, which the decisions keep. The detail stays one link away for a person; a chat is given the
   Article without its links, as before.
2. **The 276 characters it freed go to one house-rule paragraph in `host.chat`, for every modeller who installs
   Heron:** use the modeller's selection, or the active view for *"these"*, and ask if unclear; change only what was
   asked and report other problems unfixed; keep hosts, constraints and connections, replace nothing silently, clean
   nothing unasked; after a change, check what its reply counted. And *"keep replies short"* in its first line.
3. **Article 9 says where *Ask to Keep* is: in Heron's Settings, with [D-150](D-150.md) named** - it said *"on the
   ribbon"*, and D-150 moved the switch into the Settings window on 2026-11-07. That was
   [row 5b-1533](../fragment-issues/section-5b-rows-1526-1550.md), the owner's to amend, and it is amended here. The
   Enforcement table's *"the ribbon's Ask to Keep"* goes the same way.
4. **Article 12c loses its one code word.** *"Never trust a read across an `ExternalEvent` boundary"* becomes *"Never
   trust a read from an earlier step"* - what [Golden Rule 21](../14-golden-rules.md) means by it: the modeller edits,
   someone syncs, a link reloads, or an earlier step changed it, so every step re-reads. The re-count before an
   accepted preview runs, and 247 never becoming 261, are unchanged.
5. **Nothing in it is the owner's own office's way of working** - his words, 2026-11-28: *"make it this as a comenen
   not like as per my istruction liek my company istructn"*.
