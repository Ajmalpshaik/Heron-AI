### Context

D-101 proposed that a QUESTION should never be offered a fragment that writes, and left it unbuilt for
want of *"a reliable test of is this a question"*. The cost of waiting was measured on 2026-10-17: of the
120 plain questions frozen from rows 477 and 478, **32** reached a write on the trained backend and **27**
on lexical; of the owner's own 79, **11** and **9** were handed a change. Declaring sentences card by card
had already taken a further 23 off writes the same day, and still left 9 and 13.

### Decision

1. **`heron_lookup` takes `question`.** The host sets it true when the user is finding something out and
   wants nothing in the model changed. The rule is in the tool's own description, word for word as it
   was measured below - the shipped wording, within D-118's budget. False is the default, and false is
   exactly the lookup as it was before this decision - so the host leaves it false whenever it is unsure.
2. **With `question` true, every fragment above the ceiling is left out before ranking**, the way the
   Revit version wall leaves out the wrong release: not ranked lower, not offered. The identity route is
   held to it too, so a write that declares the question's exact words falls through to what is left.
3. **The ceiling is the level just below the change door's own**, read from the tool registry -
   `revit_change`'s declared risk, minus one. Today that is EXECUTE: selecting and zooming stay, and
   MODIFY, PUBLISH and ADMIN go.
4. **What was left out is said** on every answer to a question; and when nothing below the ceiling
   matches, the answer is *"Nothing in Heron that leaves the model unchanged matches that"* - the
   honest refusal D-101 asked for, rather than the nearest thing that acts.

### Why the write line and not READ

D-101 wrote *"above READ"*. On the owner's 79, with the answer key standing in for a host that is never
wrong, a READ ceiling left **2** of his questions handed a change on each backend and the write line
left **0**, with no first place lost either way. His *"Zoom to that VCD."* wants an EXECUTE, and the write line keeps it. (Isolating was the
example here until FRAGMENT-ISSUES row 5b-499, merged the same day, made `ISOLATE_ELEMENTS` a MODIFY -
it needs Heron's transaction - so a question is not offered it; the temporary isolate is `revit_isolate_temporary`'s.)

### The reliable test, measured before this was built

A fresh agent, given only the tool's description and 349 sentences in shuffled order, and never the
answer key, set the flag on each. **It was measured on four wordings.** [D-118](../DECISIONS.md)'s budget
for the always-loaded tools left room for about seventy characters: a long one of 750 characters (his 79
handed a change 9 → 2 and 9 → 1), a short one that fits (9 → 3 and 9 → 3 - it had dropped *"selecting,
isolating or zooming is not a change"*), the short one with that sentence back (9 → 1 and 9 → 1), and
**the shipped one, which says only *"Selecting or zooming is not a change"***, because row 5b-499 made
isolating a MODIFY while this was open. With the shipped wording, on the tree after that merged
(570 fragments), it flagged **all 120** plain questions, **32 of his 79**, and **1 of 150** sentences
sampled from the write cards' own utterances - *"is this grid 2d or 3d here"*, declared by
`SET_DATUM_EXTENT_TYPE`, a question by the rule's words. One of the 32 has an answer that is a change
now: #6, *"Bring back all the VCDs with the bigger counts"*, whose answer key says `ISOLATE_ELEMENTS`,
re-rated MODIFY by row 5b-499. With those labels, on his 79:

| | lexical | model |
|---|---|---|
| handed a change, flag off → the host's flags | 8 → **1** | 8 → **1** |
| right answer first | 18 → 18 | 17 → 17 |
| right answer in the top three | 27 → 29 | 28 → 32 |
| right answer lost from the shortlist | 1 - #2, GROUP_AND_COUNT, read to read | 0 |

### Alternatives considered

- **Warn only, as before**, and keep declaring reads one card at a time. It leaves a question to ranking
  wherever no card declares it, which is most sentences anybody says.
- **Heron reads the first word**, or a `?`. Against D-01, and measured worse: it flags his *"How many
  sprinklers do I need?"* and *"Do the grayout for MEP in the active view."*, misses 14 of his read
  requests phrased as instructions, and lost 3 to 5 right answers from the shortlist.

### Consequences

- **Easy:** a question the host recognises cannot be handed a change, by any route.
- **Hard:** it is only as good as the host's call. The wrong call in the dangerous direction - a change
  request flagged as a question - returns a read or nothing, never a write; the wrong call the other
  way is the lookup as it was.
- **Not covered:** `revit_read` and `revit_change` with `request` look up the words without the flag.
  `revit_read` refuses anything above its own door already; `revit_change` is for changes.
  `tools/score-routing.py` still measures the flag-off path, the one a host gets when it says nothing.
- **Locks in:** a second argument on `heron_lookup`, pinned by `tests/test_mcp_serves.py`.
