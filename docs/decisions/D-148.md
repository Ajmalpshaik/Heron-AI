### Context

D-128 left `revit_read`'s words path out, because the read door already refuses anything above its own
risk. The field report shows why that was not enough: the refusal comes **after** ranking. On Villa 1,
*"read parameter values of the elements"* ranked `SET_PARAMETER_VALUES_BY_ID` (MODIFY) first, the door
refused it, and `READ_ELEMENT_TABLE` - the read that did the job - came second and was never offered as
the answer. *"describe the selected roofs with their level, base offset, slope and thickness"* ranked
`SET_FRAMING_JUSTIFICATION` (MODIFY) first the same way.

### Decision

1. **`revit_read` with `request` looks the words up with `question` true**, exactly as `heron_lookup` does
   when the host sets it. Every fragment above D-128's ceiling is left out before ranking.
2. **Its handed-back answer says so**, D-128 item 4: how many fragments that change the model were not
   offered, and that a change goes through `revit_change`. When nothing below the ceiling matches, it
   says *"Nothing in Heron that leaves the model unchanged matches"* rather than *"no way of doing"*.
3. **Only `revit_read`.** `revit_change` is for changes. `revit_show` was outside what the report asked
   for and is left as it was.

### Evidence, this container, 2026-11-07

Not a proof under D-30: no Revit ran. The routing was measured on this container's backend, and a PC's
may rank differently.

- *"read parameter values of the elements"* - before: `SET_PARAMETER_VALUES_BY_ID` (MODIFY) first; after:
  `READ_ELEMENT_TABLE` (READ) first.
- *"describe the selected roofs ..."* - after: no write is offered; `READ_ELEMENT_LEVEL` first. That
  `READ_ELEMENT_TABLE` is not in the top five for these words is a gap in the card's words, not in this
  decision, and is left for its own change.
- `tests/test_mcp_serves.py` checks `revit_read` passes `question` true and `revit_change` false. With
  the change taken out, those checks fail (2 FAIL); with it, the suite passes.

### Consequences

- **Easy:** a read asked through `revit_read` can no longer lose its answer behind a write the door
  would refuse anyway.
- **Hard:** a read the ceiling leaves out cannot be reached by words through this door. There is none:
  the ceiling is above every risk `revit_read` itself can run.
