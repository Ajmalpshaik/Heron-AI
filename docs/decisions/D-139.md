### Context

Each of the four parts has said since it was written that it is the owner's verbatim intent, and that a change
is recorded as a decision rather than made in the file. On 2026-10-18 every section was checked against what is
built ([#668](https://github.com/Ajmalpshaik/Heron-AI/pull/668)) and a dated *As built* note was put under each
section that no longer held, with its evidence. The check also found mistakes in the parts themselves, of two
kinds:

- **Wrong when written.** 00d §8 gives Revit 2020 to 2024 one .NET Framework; 2020 is 4.7.2 and 2021 to 2024
  are 4.8. 00c names 2023 as an API break; the breaks Heron compiles around are at 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025, 2026
  and 2027.
- **Settled since by an accepted decision, a Golden Rule or an Article.** Intent and persona decided inside
  Heron, where [D-01](../DECISIONS.md#d-01--execution-host-claude-code-plugin) gives them to the host; promotion by a count of
  runs, where [D-30](../DECISIONS.md#d-30--a-fragment-is-promoted-by-one-recorded-proof-not-by-a-count-of-runs) asks for one
  recorded proof; components built on the modeller's PC, where
  [D-91](../DECISIONS.md#d-91--product-files-come-from-a-signed-versioned-github-release) and
  [D-96](../DECISIONS.md#d-96--a-downloaded-heron-is-installed-by-the-installer-and-only-by-the-installer) install a release;
  Part 3 named authoritative on fifteen Golden Rules while twenty-one are binding
  ([Q-19](../open-questions/answered.md)).

A note under a section is read after the section. A reader who skims, and a session that reads the part to
find out how Heron works, meet the wrong words first and may stop there.

### Decision

1. **A word in the four parts is corrected in place** when it is wrong as a fact that a file, a command or
   Revit's API shows, or when an Accepted decision, a Golden Rule or a Constitution Article already says
   otherwise. Nothing else is corrected under this decision.
2. **The original stays, struck through,** directly before its correction: `~~as written~~ **as corrected**`.
   A diagram that is wrong is folded, whole and unchanged, into a `<details>` block titled *Original diagram,
   as written*, and the corrected diagram stands where it was.
3. **The section's dated note says what was corrected, why, and on what evidence,** citing this decision -
   a decision, a rule, a file or a command, never a count typed by hand.
4. **A policy the owner has not chosen is not corrected.** Where two parts disagree and no accepted decision
   picks between them - the status vocabulary (D-14 is Proposed), Constitution Article 13 beside
   [D-138](../DECISIONS.md#d-138--the-keep-or-discard-question-gets-a-switch-on-the-ribbon-and-its-box-can-keep-the-rest-of-one-job-without-asking)'s
   job grant, a level for deleting and purging, one list of knowledge scopes, one priority ladder - the words
   stay as written, the note says the choice is open, and the choice is put to the owner as an open question.
   Code that picked one already does not make the pick: a rule is never edited to match code.
5. **Every later change still follows the banner:** a decision first, then the correction made as in 2 and 3.

### Alternatives considered

- **Notes only, as #668 left it.** Every fact is there, but after the wrong words. Rejected because the owner
  asked for the mistakes to be fixed, and a skim reads the words, not the notes.
- **Rewrite the parts to say what is built.** The intent would be lost, and what is built already has its own
  pages: [02 §7a](../02-architecture-overview.md#7a-as-built--the-parts-that-run-today), the
  [README](../../README.md) and the [project map](../PROJECT-MAP.md).
- **Delete the wrong lines.** Loses what the owner asked for and why; every reference to a section's words
  would point at something no longer there.

### Consequences

- **A diff of a part is no longer insertions only.** A changed line keeps its old words struck through, so the
  file itself and `git log -p` both still show them.
- **A correction can go stale as the code moves,** as a note can. The note's date says when it was checked.
- **Each part still says what Heron was asked to be.** Where the original intent and an accepted decision
  differ, the decision is what the corrected words now say, and the struck-through words what was first asked.
