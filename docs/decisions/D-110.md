### Context

[F42](../proposals/f42.md) item 5, and row 1 of [41 §12](../41-hvac-design.md): the owner's own AJ-Tools knowledge
file puts a supply diffuser's neck at **2.5 m/s for NC/RC 30**, while ASHRAE HVAC Applications (2019)
Ch. 49 Table 9, as transcribed, gives **2.2 m/s** for a supply outlet at RC 30 - its 2.5 is the RC 35 row,
or a return opening at RC 30. Heron had recorded both and resolved neither.

### Decision

1. **2.5 m/s governs** a supply diffuser's neck in an NC/RC 30 room. It is the owner's figure - not a
   standard's, and not Heron's.
2. **Only where its condition holds.** `diffuser_select` takes the room's criterion as `max_nc`; at 30 the
   neck limit is 2.5 m/s. At any other criterion nothing is applied: the limit is asked, with ASHRAE's row
   for that criterion offered beside the question.
3. **Said out loud every time it is used** - the answer names the figure as the office's own, cites this
   decision, and names the 2.2 it replaces.
4. **A figure the modeller states wins.** Given `max_neck_velocity_ms`, the engine uses it as given.
5. **ASHRAE's table stays as ASHRAE prints it.** The office's figure sits beside it, and the table's note
   says so.

### What it accepts

- **It is the owner's, so it goes with the owner to every project.** The day a second office uses Heron it
  is theirs to replace - revisit on the first multi-user install, the reservation D-83 already holds.
- **NC/RC 30 only.** The AJ-Tools file states it for that criterion, and a figure applied past the
  condition it was given for is the *remembered answer applied to a job it does not fit* that D-33 names
  as the failure to watch for.

### Consequences

- **Easy:** an NC/RC 30 room's necks are sized without a question.
- **Owed:** nothing in Revit - the engine reads no model and this changes no fragment.
