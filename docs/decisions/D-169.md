### Context

The DISCLAIMER's *On standards checking* said: *"Where Heron reports compliance with a standard - ISO 19650,
QCS, Ashghal requirements, a company standard - it reports against the source documents it has been given,
and cites them. It does not certify compliance."* The instruction every chat reads,
[`host.chat.yaml`](../../brain/instructions/host.chat.yaml), says: *"A check that passes means the model meets
the values that were checked, and that is what you say. Never call a model, a system or a design compliant -
whether it complies is the engineer's or the authority's decision."* Both say Heron certifies nothing. They
differed in one word: the page a company reads before using Heron said it *reports compliance*, a word the
product is told never to say ([Q-79](../open-questions/tier-2.md)).

### Decision

1. **The paragraph reads:** *"Where Heron checks a model against a standard - ISO 19650, QCS, Ashghal
   requirements, a company standard - it reports which of the checked values the model meets and which it
   does not, against the source documents it has been given, and cites them."* Then, as before:
   ***"It does not certify compliance."*** It does not replace a competent person's review, a formal QA/QC
   process, or your obligations under the project's information requirements.
2. **The page says what it said before**, in one sentence under the paragraph, as it does for the two
   earlier corrections it records.
3. **The chat's instruction does not change.** It was already the stricter wording; the DISCLAIMER now
   matches it.

### Alternatives considered

- **Leave both** (Q-79 shape 2). Rejected: the page a company reads before using Heron would go on saying a
  word the product is told never to say.

### Consequences

- **The word *compliance* stays on the page only where Heron disclaims it**: *"It does not certify
  compliance."*
- **An engineer or an authority reads the wording a tool is expected to give** - values met, not compliance
  granted.
- **Only the owner changes this page**, and this paragraph was changed on his answer.
