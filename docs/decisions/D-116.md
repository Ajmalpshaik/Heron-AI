### Context

Most of a family build's time is the chat's own turns, not Revit's: [04](../04-heron-mcp.md) records a
165-form family at 1,423 s with the model's turns 95% of it, and Revit's own time over that day's 198
fragment runs at 23.5 s. One form a call is what multiplies the turns. `CREATE_FAMILY_FORMS`
(FRG-GEO-060, DRAFT) was built on 2026-10-11 as one shape for the owner to judge - a list of extrusions
and revolves in one call - and [Q-62](../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md) left him two choices that shape makes.

### Decision

1. **A whole list undoes as one.** One `CREATE_FAMILY_FORMS` call is one entry in Revit's undo list,
   however many rows it holds. A modeller who wants finer undo steps gets them from how the chat splits
   the family - the body in one list, the nozzles in a second, the base in a third - one undo each.
2. **Cuts stay a separate call.** A void is cut into a solid by `COMBINE_FAMILY_FORMS`, naming the ids
   `CREATE_FAMILY_FORMS` gave back. `CREATE_FAMILY_FORMS` takes no cut field.

### Alternatives considered

- **One undo per form.** Means one form per call, and keeps the turns the measurement found.
- **Cuts in the same call.** `COMBINE_FAMILY_FORMS` already takes many ids in one call - one call per
  body, not per hole - so it saves about one call per body. It would widen a DRAFT fragment before its
  first proof, and a cut Revit refuses would cost the whole list of forms with it.

### Consequences

- **No code changes.** The fragment as written already undoes as one and leaves cutting to
  `COMBINE_FAMILY_FORMS`; Group DA's DA2 is the run that shows the one undo entry.
- **This decides the shape, not the proof.** `create-family-forms` stays DRAFT until Group DA has run in
  Revit and its proof is signed.
- **Undo is as coarse as the list.** A modeller who undoes a list undoes all of it; the chat chooses how
  coarse by how it splits the family.
