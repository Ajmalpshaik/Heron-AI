### Context

Finalize ([docs/44](../44-building-loads-from-the-model.md) section 6) writes Design Cooling Load, Design Heating Load
and Specified Supply Airflow into every calculated Space through `SET_PARAMETER_VALUES_BY_ID`, then each Space's flow
across its diffusers through `SET_AIR_TERMINAL_FLOW`, after `FILTER_ELEMENTS_BY_CATEGORY` hands the air terminals over,
and makes a Spaces schedule once with `CREATE_SCHEDULE`. Each write is its own `revit_change`, so the modeller's undo
list gains two entries, three with a new schedule, and the panel says so. Article 8 asks for one.

One `TransactionGroup` cannot span two requests: each request is one job on Revit's thread, and a group is opened
and closed inside it. The add-in already runs several cards inside one group - a proof's **setup steps**
(`RevitFragment.RunSetupSteps`), each committed in a transaction of its own inside the group of the card that follows
them - but only `prove` and `validate` send them, and only for a run that is rolled back. A chat's `revit_change`
carries one card. So one undo entry is a change to what the write door accepts, not something the server can do by
calling `revit_change` twice.

### Decision (proposed)

1. **A change the SERVER builds from several cards goes to Revit as one request**: the earlier cards as setup steps of
   the last, all inside its one named `TransactionGroup`, answered by one Keep or Discard and left as one entry in
   Revit's undo list. The first such job is Finalize: the Space values, then the air terminals handed over, then the
   diffusers' flows - and the schedule, when it is made, as a step too.
2. **Every step is gated as if it were sent alone**, in the add-in, before anything runs: the highest risk of any step
   decides which switch must be on; a step that deletes is asked through the delete box; the read-back covers what
   each step was handed; and a refusal, a stale row or a Revit failure in any step rolls the whole request back.
3. **A chat cannot build such a request from `revit_change`'s arguments.** The steps are named in the server's own
   code for a job of its own, so no sentence a modeller types becomes a chain of writes nobody previewed as one.

### Alternatives considered

- **One card that writes the Space values and the diffusers' flows together** - `SET_AIR_TERMINAL_FLOW` widened, or a
  card of its own. One fragment, one group, no change to the door. But it would carry a second copy of
  `SET_PARAMETER_VALUES_BY_ID`'s job and its stale-row check (Golden Rule 3), and a PROVEN card widened goes back to
  DRAFT until it is proved again.
- **Leave it as it is**, two or three entries, said beside the button and in the answer. Honest, and what the panel
  does today; it is the Article 8 exception this decision exists to end.
- **A group the bridge holds open across several requests.** Revit runs each request as its own event; a group left
  open between them is open while the modeller works, and a crash between two requests leaves it open.

### Consequences

- Finalize is undone with one Ctrl+Z, and Discard in Revit's box takes back the Space values and the diffusers
  together, where today the diffusers are written only after the Space values were kept.
- The add-in's setup steps stop being a proving tool only: what they may do on a kept change is gated as in point 2,
  and needs a Revit run - CC11 counts the undo entries again, and a negative case: a stale Space row refuses the
  whole request with no diffuser written.
- Until the owner answers, nothing changes: Finalize keeps its separate entries and says how many it made.
