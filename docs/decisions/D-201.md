### Context

Heron's search had an optional third stage: after word match and meaning search were fused, a
cross-encoder (`sentence-transformers`, `cross-encoder/ms-marco-MiniLM-L-6-v2`) re-read the top 20
(question, card) pairs and re-ordered them. It was built as Stage 7 of the RAG plan and measured four
times. The figures, every one recorded before this decision, are in
[`brain/retrieval-history.md`](../../brain/retrieval-history.md):

- **2026-09-24, 398 fragments, his 79 questions:** first place **19 on the model, 12 with the
  re-ranker**; a question that asked for no change handed one, **9 and 15**.
- **2026-10-17, after [D-134](D-134.md), 571 fragments** - the re-ranker given each card's first eight
  sentences: on the owner's backend first place **17** and top three **27**, against the model alone's
  **17** and **28** the same day; a question handed a change **11**, against **8** on the model alone.
  The 120 plain questions answered by a change: **24** with it (D-134's record), **9** on the model
  alone the same day at 569 fragments. No clear gain.
- **Cost:** about **+80 ms a question** on one thread after D-134 (D-134's own record); the largest
  optional download in Heron - a torch build, **500 MB to 2 GB** by the field reading it printed; and
  on the owner's 15.8 GB laptop, with the model and the re-ranker installed, each open chat's Heron held
  **2.0 to 2.3 GB** (2026-10-11, [WHAT-TO-INSTALL](../WHAT-TO-INSTALL.md)).
- **Who had it:** Setup ([`PythonSetup.cs`](../../platform/Heron.Installer/PythonSetup.cs)) installs only
  from `requirements-lock.txt`, which holds neither the re-ranker nor model2vec. **Modellers never had
  the re-ranker; only the owner's PC did** - so its rankings were a backend no other PC and no CI run
  shared, and card wording was tuned against it (rows 5b-467 to 5b-510, 5b-1293).

Measured again on 2026-11-26 at **753 fragments** in a cloud container with model2vec and no re-ranker
(`tools/score-routing.py`), his 79 questions: first place **23** on the built-in backend and **20** on
the model, top three **32** and **31**, a question handed a change **9** and **6**; the 124 plain
questions reaching a write **9** and **15**.

### Decision

1. **The cross-encoder re-ranker is removed** - the module, its test, its warm-up, its line in every
   lookup answer, its store table, its backends in `score-routing`, and `sentence-transformers` from
   `requirements-optional.txt`. Fusion's order is the order shown.
2. **model2vec stays, and stays optional.** It is not added to `requirements-lock.txt` or to Setup: on
   the figures above it is not clearly better than the built-in backend: it hands fewer of his questions
   a change (6 against 9), but puts fewer first (20 against 23) and sends more plain questions to a
   write (15 against 9) - and it needs
   `huggingface.co` once for its weights, which a locked-down PC may refuse. The built-in backend is what
   every modeller has.
3. **`heron_embed` loads model2vec or nothing.** Its fallback to the same second library, as an encoder
   nobody measured, goes with it.
4. **On the owner's PC:** `pip uninstall -y sentence-transformers` (torch with it, if nothing else of his
   needs torch). Nothing else changes there: model2vec stays.

### Alternatives considered

- **Keep it, off by default.** It already was - absent everywhere but one PC - and that is the problem:
  a backend one machine ranks on and nothing else measures is where card wording drifted.
- **Remove model2vec too.** Not chosen by the owner. It costs about 130 MB and nothing in memory beyond
  the chat, and it is the only meaning-aware search Heron has.
- **Make the re-ranker read documents only.** An open idea in WHAT-TO-INSTALL; nobody built it, and its
  one measured document question is not enough to keep a 2 GB install for.

### Consequences

- **On a PC without the re-ranker every answer is the one it was.** Measured the same day on this
  container, before and after the change: `tools/score-routing.py` on the model and on the built-in
  backend, and `tests/test_routing_phrases.py` on both - see the 2026-11-26 entry in
  `brain/retrieval-history.md`.
- **On the owner's PC the answers become the model's.** `tests/test_routing_phrases.py` fails 6 checks
  there (row 5b-1882) - the same 6 it failed in this container before the change. They are card wording,
  owed in their rows; no card was edited here.
- **The order of a card's utterances no longer matters to ranking.** Nothing reads only the first eight.
- **Every knowledge store is re-indexed once** (`INDEX_FORMAT` 4), and drops the `rerank_said` table.
- **Register rows and decisions that name the re-ranker are history and keep their words.**
- Nothing here touches Revit. Not run in Revit.
