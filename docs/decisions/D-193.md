### Context

Two cards made sheets. `CREATE_SHEETS` (PROVEN, version 3) took typed numbers and names, or copied the sheets handed
in; `CREATE_SHEETS_FROM_TABLE` (PROVEN, version 1) took a table from the modeller's spreadsheet, with a title block per
row and every other column filled in. The duplicate check of row 5b-1875 found them one job as two tools, and the
merge rows 5b-1486 and 5b-1875 planned was the table INTO `CREATE_SHEETS` as its version 4. Three rules the two
cards held opposite had to be one rule before they could be one card:

- a sheet NUMBER or NAME Revit refuses after the sheet is made - `CREATE_SHEETS` kept the sheet (in `refused` when its
  number did not take, and with no word at all when only its name did not, row 5b-1893); the table card made each
  sheet in a SubTransaction of its own and took that one back;
- the same number twice in one request - `CREATE_SHEETS` made the first and refused the second as already used; the
  table card made neither;
- the 1,000-sheet ceiling - the table card's; typed numbers had none.

On 2026-11-21 the owner said *"merge: yes"* on #1080 and left the three to the session: *"remaining all the things
you can choose what is the best"*.

### Decision

1. **A sheet whose number or name Revit refuses is taken back** - not made, and listed in `notCreated` with Revit's own
   words - so no sheet is left in the register under a number or a name Revit chose by itself. Each sheet is made in
   a SubTransaction of its own; what Revit THROWS takes that one sheet back. A failure Revit only posts as the call is
   kept still rolls the whole call back, one undo (Article 8).
2. **The same number twice in one request, capitals ignored, makes neither sheet**, and each entry is listed - which
   was meant is the modeller's to say (D-33).
3. **One call makes at most 1,000 sheets** - a table's rows, and typed numbers too. More is refused whole; nothing is
   made.
4. **All three hold for a table and for typed numbers alike**, on `CREATE_SHEETS` version 4. D-183's four choices hold
   as they were: a blank cell writes nothing, an Excel date goes in as an ISO date, a parameter VALUE Revit refuses is
   listed while its sheet is kept - rule 1 is about the sheet's own number and name - and 1,000 rows is the most.

### Alternatives considered

Keeping a sheet Revit refused and listing it (`CREATE_SHEETS`' old rule: the register then holds a sheet nobody asked
for under a number or name Revit picked); making the first of two equal numbers (a guess at which was meant); no
ceiling for typed numbers (two rules for one job). In each the table card's rule was the safer, and it was taken.

### Consequences

- `CREATE_SHEETS` is DRAFT at version 4 until one proving run on the owner's PC - [Group OZ](../needs-checking/group-oz.md),
  a positive and a negative case for both routes and each of the three rules. Until then *make a new sheet*, *duplicate
  these sheets* and *make the sheets from this Excel* are previewed from the command line and run once through
  `try-once` (D-117, D-119), not through `revit_change`.
- `CREATE_SHEETS_FROM_TABLE` is DEPRECATED, superseded by `CREATE_SHEETS`; its proof and its Group MY stay as the record.
- The copy job keeps version 3's rule - a copy whose number did not take is kept and named, because it exists - until
  the owner says whether rule 1 reaches it ([row 5b-1935](../fragment-issues/row-5b-1935.md)).
