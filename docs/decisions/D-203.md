### Context

After [D-201](D-201.md) removed the re-ranker earlier the same day, Heron had two searches for the
nearness route: the built-in character n-grams every PC has, and model2vec (`minishlab/potion-base-8M`),
used where it was installed. Setup ([`PythonSetup.cs`](../../platform/Heron.Installer/PythonSetup.cs))
installs only from `requirements-lock.txt`, which holds neither - so, as with the re-ranker, **only the
owner's PC ranked on the model**, and card wording was tuned against a search no modeller and no CI run
shared. [`tests/test_routing_phrases.py`](../../tests/test_routing_phrases.py) refused to run anywhere
but on the model (exit 3), so its guards ran on one PC only.

Measured on 2026-11-26 by the session that proposed this, at **753 fragments**, with the card wording of
[#1424](https://github.com/Ajmalpshaik/Heron-AI/pull/1424) (branch `session/S3-card-routing`),
`tools/score-routing.py`, built in against the model:

| | Built in | Model |
|---|---|---|
| The owner's 79: right capability first | **34** | 30 |
| ... in the top three | **43** | 37 |
| ... not found | **18** | 21 |
| ... asked for no change, handed one | 7 | **6** |
| ... asked for one change, handed another | 14 | **12** |
| The 124 plain questions handed a change | **9** | 15 |
| `tests/test_document_retrieval.py` | 27 of 27 | 27 of 27 |
| A lookup, median | **109 ms** | 147 ms |

D-201's own run the same morning, on main's wording at 753 fragments, had the same shape: first place 23
and 20, top three 32 and 31, a question handed a change 9 and 6, plain questions to a write 9 and 15.

### Decision

1. **model2vec is removed** - the trained branch of `heron_embed`, its warm-up and the store warm-up's
   wait for it, its size and install announcement, the tokenizer count of a card's opening (`reach()`,
   `cut_before_opening()`, `MODEL_READS`), its `model` weight in fusion, the `model` backend in
   `score-routing`, its lines in `heron-ready`, `check-dependencies`, `measure-brain` and the cloud setup
   script, and model2vec and huggingface-hub from `requirements-optional.txt`.
2. **Heron has one search, built in, the same on every PC.** `heron_embed.backend()` always answers
   `lexical` and still says so on every answer; nothing is downloaded for search.
3. **The built-in search gives the same answers it gave before.** Nothing in its code or its fusion
   weight changes; measured the same day, below.
4. **`tests/test_routing_phrases.py` runs on the built-in search**, everywhere, CI included. Its guards
   are fixed by card wording where they can be, never by editing a guard (D-100); one that cannot be is
   recorded, not weakened.
5. **On the owner's PC:** `pip uninstall -y model2vec` (and its weights in
   `%USERPROFILE%\.cache\huggingface` may be deleted). Nothing else changes there: every answer becomes
   the one a PC without the model already gave.

### Alternatives considered

- **Keep it optional, as D-201 decided that morning.** That is the one-PC search D-201 removed the
  re-ranker for: a backend only the owner ranks on, which card wording drifts towards and no CI run
  measures. On the figures above it is not better.
- **Install it everywhere, through Setup.** It would make the model the one search, but it is behind on
  first place, top three and plain questions, slower per lookup, and needs `huggingface.co` once - which
  a locked-down PC may refuse.

### Consequences

- **On a PC without model2vec every answer is the one it was.** `tools/score-routing.py` on the owner's
  79 and the 124 plain questions, and nine frozen sentence sets with `--sentences --compare`, gave the
  same answer to every sentence before (main, the model forced off) and after - see the 2026-11-26
  entry in [`brain/retrieval-history.md`](../../brain/retrieval-history.md).
- **On the owner's PC the answers become the built-in search's.** The figures above are what that moves.
- **Every knowledge store re-embeds a model-stamped row once**, the first time it opens: the stamp is
  the encoder's name, and `lexical` no longer matches `model:...`.
- **`tests/test_routing_phrases.py` on the built-in search:** nine guards failed, the same nine on
  `main`; card wording repaired two, and the seven left are recorded open in
  [row 5b-2053](../fragment-issues/row-5b-2053.md) (issue #1440) for the owner, one at a time - reword or
  close as by design. Until then the suite asks every guard and exits 3, waiting, and CI keeps it on its
  not-runnable list; any other failure exits 1.
- **Owed checks that asked for a `model` run now owe a `lexical` one**, re-pointed in their rows.
- **Register rows and decisions that name the model are history and keep their words.**
- Nothing here touches Revit. Not run in Revit.
