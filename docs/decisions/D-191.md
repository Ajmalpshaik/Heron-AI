### Context

D-70 decided Heron keeps a counter - a clause and a number, on the PC, per scope, outside the disposable
store, never sent - and left four things open: what exactly is counted, what counts as *helped*, how much
it may move an answer, and whether the question's words are kept. R-25 could not be built until they were.
The four were put to the owner as one recommendation on #1096, and he said yes to all four.

### Decision

1. **What it counts, per clause:** the times Heron showed it to the chat (*offered*), the times it helped
   (*helped*), and the last date it helped.
2. **"Helped" is only the modeller's own word** - said after an answer, through the `heron_helped` tool or
   the command line. Heron never infers it.
3. **It moves a clause less than one rank:** helped over offered, times half a fusion rank. It settles a
   tie and never beats a match the routes ranked higher. The date is kept and shown, and not weighted.
4. **The question's words are never kept** - only the clause id and the counts.

### Consequences

- The counter is one small file beside each store, `<scope>-usage.json`, never inside it, so deleting a
  store keeps it (Golden Rule 11); `python brain/heron_usage.py --forget` deletes it.
- The release builder refuses a `-usage.json` file, as it refuses a store: it never leaves the PC (D-70).
- Fragments are not counted: the counter is about clauses, as D-70's example was.
- **Where a re-ranker is installed, its score sets the order**, and the nudge sits in the tie-break after
  it: it decides only between clauses the cross-encoder scored the same. It is never added to the
  cross-encoder's score, whose scale is the model's own (found on the owner's PC, 2026-11-20).
