### Context

`revit_change`'s outcome line said KEPT whenever the add-in's verdict was `kept`, and the table said so in words:
KEPT is *"not a claim that anything changed: the result lines are that"*. Two runs on Revit 2024 showed what that
costs a chat told to go by the line. On 2026-11-13 `CREATE_LEVELS` asked for a name already taken answered
`created` 0 and *"refused 1 name(s) already taken, so NO LEVEL WAS CREATED"* - and `outcome: KEPT` (row 5b-1635).
On 2026-11-14 `EDIT_TEXT_VALUES` asked to replace a text no view held changed nothing, Revit raised no change event,
its undo list rightly had no entry - and the line read KEPT (row 5b-1657). D-160 rolls back a keep Revit WITNESSED
counting zero; a keep Revit raised no event for was kept as before, because silence is not proof that nothing
changed. The owner was asked both on 2026-11-15 and answered both.

### Decision

1. **A change the add-in answered `kept` reads `NOTHING CHANGED` when nothing changed by every count Heron has,
   and `REFUSED` when, beside that, the capability's own `refused` holds something.** `NOTHING CHANGED` is a ninth
   word in `heron_server_core.OUTCOMES`, after KEPT: Revit kept the transaction, nothing in the model changed, and
   there is nothing to undo.
2. **Every count, or KEPT.** All of these must hold, read by `kept_nothing`:
   - Revit's change report is in the reply, carrying `events` and `witnessed`, none of its events was unreadable, and it counted nothing - no event
     at all, or a total of 0 - with no element added, modified or deleted beside it. A reply from an add-in that
     sends no report is not read as empty.
   - The card declares at least one `role: result`, every one of them came back, and every one reads zero, as
     `heron_validate.looks_empty` reads a proof's negative leg. A card that declares no result has nothing a zero
     could be read off.
   - Nothing could not be read: a value Heron cannot judge, or a module it cannot import, leaves KEPT.
3. **A partial result is KEPT.** Anything found, made or changed - eleven pairs joined and the twelfth refused - is
   KEPT, whatever `refused` says. Saying REFUSED or NOTHING CHANGED of a call that changed something would be worse
   than the fault it repairs.
4. **The activity list follows the word:** a card's own refusal is `refused` there, as every other REFUSED is.
   Nothing is rolled back and nothing in Revit changes: this is what the reply says, never what Revit does. D-160's
   rollback of a witnessed empty keep is unchanged and still reads ROLLED BACK.

### What is not known

Nothing of it has run against a real Revit: `tests/test_outcome_line.py` drives the door against a stand-in Revit
with a real card's declared results, and each of its five new cases - refused and nothing made, nothing changed,
part done, Revit counting a change, an unreadable event - says its word. Whether every card's `refused` and
results reach the reply as the stand-in sends them is the PC's: rows 5b-1635 and 5b-1657 owe the two runs again,
`CREATE_LEVELS` with a taken name and `EDIT_TEXT_VALUES` with a text no view holds, on Revit 2024.

### Alternatives considered

- **Roll an un-witnessed empty keep back, as D-160 does a witnessed one.** It would read ROLLED BACK - true of what
  Heron did, but it needs the add-in to know which results a card declares, which it does not, and a rebuild and
  restart of every Revit; and the undo list is already right.
- **Keep KEPT and say more in the table.** The table already said it, and a chat still read KEPT as "it worked".
- **Read the words of the reply.** The line exists so that nobody has to (row 5b-1159).

### Consequences

A chat told to go by the outcome line is no longer told KEPT for a call that made nothing. A card whose results are
not declared with `role: result`, or that names its refusals other than `refused`, still reads KEPT - the rule
fails closed, toward the old word.
