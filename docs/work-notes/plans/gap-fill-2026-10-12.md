# Gap fill: the jobs another plugin's tool list showed Heron could not do (2026-10-12)

**Active.** Owner: the session building it, on the owner's instruction of 2026-10-12 to keep building until
the list is done. **Retires when every row below is built or struck with a reason**, the durable half
then living in the cards themselves and in their NEEDS-CHECKING groups.

## Where the list came from

On 2026-10-12 the owner asked for another Revit AI plugin's published tool list to be checked against
Heron. Every one of its tools was compared with Heron's library by the job it does, not by its name. The
comparison is kept outside the repository, and the other product is deliberately not named here or in any
card ([31](../../31-studying-the-existing-libraries.md): mechanisms and lessons are re-authored, never
imported). Nothing of its code, names or wording is used: each job below is described in Heron's words and
built the way Heron builds.

That day's result, as a dated snapshot and not a count to maintain: of 421 tools, 189 did a job Heron
already did, 121 did a job Heron did in part, and 105 did a job Heron had no tool for.

## The owner's rules for this work

1. **General, not one job.** Not "move a duct" but "move elements". The case in hand goes in as a value.
2. **Widen an existing fragment when one nearly does the job.** A new capability only when the job is new.
3. **One fragment can cover several of the other plugin's tools.** Seven off-axis warning fixers are one
   job here: straighten lines that are slightly off an axis.
4. **No one-time tools.** A job done once per project, or handled by a skill or method, is not made a
   fragment.
5. Every new fragment is **DRAFT** until proved on a named test model with a negative case (D-30).
   Widening a PROVEN fragment makes its proof stale, so those wait for the owner's yes.

## Not built, and why

| Job | Why not |
|---|---|
| Undo | By design: one Heron request is one entry in Revit's own Undo list, and the modeller presses it |
| Ping, calculator, session start, connection recovery | Not a model job. `revit_health` answers the connection question |
| Read or press a Revit dialog's buttons | A dialog is the modeller's to answer |
| Turn on worksharing | Once per project |
| Read or fill an office drawing-register spreadsheet | A spreadsheet job for a skill, not the model |
