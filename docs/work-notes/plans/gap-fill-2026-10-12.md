# Gap fill: Revit jobs Heron could not do yet (2026-10-12)

**Active.** Owner: the session building it, on the owner's instruction of 2026-10-12 to keep building until
the list is done. **Retires when every row below is built or struck with a reason**, the durable half
then living in the cards themselves and in their NEEDS-CHECKING groups.

## What this is

On 2026-10-12 the owner listed Revit jobs Heron could not do yet, or did only in part, and asked for each
to be built the Heron way. Each job below is described in Heron's words and built as Heron builds.

## The owner's rules for this work

1. **General, not one job.** Not "move a duct" but "move elements". The case in hand goes in as a value.
2. **Widen an existing fragment when one nearly does the job.** A new capability only when the job is new.
3. **One fragment can cover several jobs.** Fixing Revit's off-axis warning on walls, grids, lines and
   the rest is one job here: straighten lines that are slightly off an axis.
4. **No one-time tools.** A job done once per project, or handled by a skill or method, is not made a
   fragment.
5. Every new fragment is **DRAFT** until proved on a named test model with a negative case (D-30).
   Widening a PROVEN fragment makes its proof stale, so those wait for the owner's yes.

## Not built, and why

| Job | Why not |
|---|---|
| Undo | By design: one Heron request is one entry in Revit's own Undo list, and the modeller presses it |
| Read or press a Revit dialog's buttons | A dialog is the modeller's to answer |
| Turn on worksharing | Once per project |
| Read or fill an office drawing-register spreadsheet | A spreadsheet job for a skill, not the model |
