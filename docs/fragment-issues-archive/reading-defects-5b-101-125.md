### Row 5b-113

*Moved from the register on 2026-11-17.*

**Defect.** **A SUB-CLAUSE LETTER TURNS A CITATION INTO AN INVENTED FACT, AND THE SAME CLAUSE SPELLED OUT GETS THE RIGHT ANSWER.** Found 2026-09-22 reading `brain/heron_ground.py`, 1,115 lines, 9 suites - the thinnest-held live-path module left part-read. A bracketed span is a CITATION or it is part of the CLAIM, and `_is_marker` decides: citation-shaped by `_CITE_SHAPED`, and not a measurement by `_MEASURED`. **`_CITE_SHAPED`'s own comment names `7a` as a locator it accepts** - *“Section 3, para-7, clause 4.1a”* and *“4, 4.1, 9.1.1, 7a”* - and `_MEASURED` then reads it as a measurement, because its letter arm is `[a-z]+` and `a` is also **amperes**. **MEASURED END TO END on one sentence and one packet holding clause 9.1.1**, verdict and added facts, citing a clause the packet does NOT carry: `[9.1.2]` gives **unresolved**, added `25mm`; **`[7a]` gives `uncited`, added `25mm` AND `7a`**; **`[4.1a]` gives `uncited`, added `25mm`, `4.1a` AND `4`**; and `[clause 4.1a]` - the same clause with one word in front - gives **unresolved**, added `25mm`. **BOTH HALVES ARE WRONG AND THE FILE SAYS WHY.** `check()` separates those two verdicts deliberately - *“TWO DIFFERENT WRONGS, AND THEY NEED DIFFERENT ANSWERS”*, R-65 for a sentence that cites nothing and R-22 for a citation a human cannot follow - and a sub-clause citation gets the other one, so the report tells a modeller who DID cite that they cited nothing. And the citation's own text is listed as a fact the source does not carry, which this module's docstring calls the failure that gets it switched off: *“every false flag it produces is a true sentence called a lie”*. **WHEN THE PACKET DOES CARRY THAT LOCATOR IT IS FINE** - `known` resolves it and the verdict is `grounded` - so this bites exactly where the check matters most: a clause the packet does not hold

**State.** **MOVED TO ISSUE [#922](https://github.com/Ajmalpshaik/Heron-AI/issues/922) 2026-11-16** - still open, tracked there; this row is kept whole. Was: **OPEN - measured and deliberately NOT fixed, because the obvious fix re-opens a door two review rounds closed.** `_MEASURED` exists as a second guard for a reason `_is_marker` states: bracketing a value turned the fabrication check off for that sentence, so `[50mm]` must stay a claim. **Narrowing its letters to Heron's own `_UNITS` does not help, and that was checked rather than assumed: `a` is amperes and `7a` still loses.** So `[7a]` is genuinely ambiguous in isolation, and the module already has a stated policy for exactly that - `_is_marker`'s note on `[99]`, *“the honest edge”*, says a bare count is both a plausible locator and a checkable fact, `known` decides it, and **where nothing resolves it, this still reads it as a MARKER**. **THE LETTERED CASE DOES THE OPPOSITE OF THE POLICY WRITTEN BESIDE IT**, and that is the conflict to settle: one sentence, whose answer decides the fix. Read an unresolved citation-shaped span as a MARKER, which is what `[99]` already does and what the sub-clause needs - then `[50mm]` with no other citation reports `unresolved` rather than `uncited`, which is the wrong one of the two wrongs for an abuse case. Or keep the measurement veto and accept that a sub-clause letter is not citable in brackets, which every standard in Qatar MEP work writes. **NOTHING WAS EDITED**, per this register's own rule: a policy conflict is left open rather than closed whichever way makes the task easier
