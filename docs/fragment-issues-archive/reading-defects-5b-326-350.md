### Row 5b-331

*Moved from the register on 2026-11-17.*

**Defect.** **THE LOADS TAKE REVIT'S LAYER-ONLY U AS AN AIR-TO-AIR U, AND ROUND PEOPLE TO WHOLE PERSONS.** Found 2026-10-06 by the hand check of Office 101 ([Group CC](../needs-checking/group-cc.md)). Revit fills a Basic Wall's, Floor's or Roof's U as 1 / (the sum of its LAYERS' resistances), with no inside or outside surface resistance - Generic - 225mm Masonry reads R 0.1731 = 0.225 m / 1.30 W/(m.K) - and the engine uses it as the whole assembly's U, where ASHRAE's U-factors include the films (Fundamentals Ch. 26). On a well-insulated wall that is 4 % (0.2306 against 0.2222); on the 100 mm blockwork partition to the unconditioned store it is 8.78 against 2.83, and Office 101's total is 4.138 kW against 3.173 kW by hand - 30 % high. Separately, 27.035 m2 x 0.05 people/m2 = 1.35 people became 1, in the people gain and in the outdoor air's Pz. Both move design figures, so how they are handled is the owner's to decide (D-33).

**State.** **MOVED TO ISSUE [#922](https://github.com/Ajmalpshaik/Heron-AI/issues/922) 2026-11-16** - still open, tracked there; this row is kept whole. Was: **OPEN - recorded for the owner, not fixed.** With Heron's own assumptions the hand check gives 4.153 kW, 0.4 % from Heron's 4.138: the arithmetic and the sun are right.
