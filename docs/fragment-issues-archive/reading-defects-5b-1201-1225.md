### Row 5b-1201

*Moved from the register on 2026-11-17.*

**Defect.** **`PLACE_STRUCTURAL_FAMILY` PLACED A BEAM AT EACH POINT, ANSWERED SUCCESS, AND REVIT THEN DELETED THE GEOMETRY OF TWO OF THEM - ZERO-LENGTH BROKEN BEAMS KEPT AND REPORTED AS PLACED.** Seen 2026-10-19 by Ajmal PS on Revit 2020.2, building a twisting tower: `PLACE_STRUCTURAL_FAMILY` (v3, PROVEN) with symbol `M_Concrete-Rectangular Beam : 400 x 800mm` (Structural Framing), `structuralType=Beam`, `level=Level 1`, points `23420,0,0; 23640.272,2142.771,4000; 23569.859,4319.298,8000` answered **placed 3, failed 0**. Revit then deleted the geometry of 2 of them (*"Line is too short"*, *"Can't create sweep"*), so the broken beams stayed in the model as a success. **A beam or a brace is driven by the line between its two ends, and a point cannot give one** - the fragment is point-based. Version 3 let a Structural Framing family through as a beam or a brace on purpose, and its own `tests/cases.yaml` said Revit refuses the point overload for a line-based family per point; on Revit 2020.2 it did not. **The right tool already exists:** `PLACE_LINE_BASED_FAMILY` (FRG-ELE-063, DRAFT) places beams and braces along `curves`, start;end pairs in millimetres. Its proof is the owner's call and is not part of this row. **Two defects in one:** the beam request was not refused, and a placement whose geometry Revit deleted was counted as placed.

Whether the keeper refuses a beam or a brace the way version 4 did was NOT read here, so the row is not closed. Was: **OPEN - fixed in source 2026-10-19 at DRAFT, version 4; a Revit run is owed ([Group HO](../needs-checking/group-ho.md)).** `place-structural-family` version 4: "beam" or "brace" asked of any family that is not a column or a foundation family, and a Structural Framing family asked as anything, is **refused before anything is placed** with a sentence naming `PLACE_LINE_BASED_FAMILY` and its `curves` form; a column or foundation family asked as a beam keeps version 3's sentence (ask for it as its own kind, row 128).
