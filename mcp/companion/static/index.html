HVAC Load Calculation

Cooling (AC) and heating load per Space · ASHRAE method · a peak estimate, not an hourly simulation like HAP

Ask the chat to "calculate the loads for this building". Heron reads every Space's walls and windows from Revit, works out each Space's cooling (AC) load and heating load, and shows the table here - you change the people, lights and equipment, press Recalculate, and write a report.

Sprinkler Hydraulic Calculation

One sprinkler system from the model · Hazen-Williams · a design aid, not a listed hydraulic program - the fire consultant and the authority approve the design

Ask the chat to "run the hydraulics on this sprinkler system". Heron reads the pipes, fittings and heads from Revit and shows them here - you confirm the K-factors, give the design criteria, tick the remote area and press Calculate.

Sprinkler Layout

The rooms you selected · a regular grid checked as NFPA 13 measures it · a design aid - the fire consultant and the authority approve the layout

Select Spaces or Rooms in Revit and ask the chat to "place sprinklers in these rooms". Heron reads them and shows each room's plan here - you answer the hazard class, the ceiling and the limits, press Preview, check the plans, and Apply one level at a time.
