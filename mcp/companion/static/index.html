    HVAC Load Calculation

    Cooling (AC) and heating load per Space · ASHRAE method · a peak estimate, not an hourly simulation like HAP

    Work out cooling and heating loads, Space by Space

    1. Ask the chat. Heron reads every Space's walls, roofs and windows from Revit.
    2. Check the 3D take-off. Look at the faces the loads are worked from, then confirm it is right.
    3. Change people, lights and equipment, press Recalculate, then write the report.
